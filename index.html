    • Setup 8 men each, on the dark squares of your first two rows. Black moves first.
    • Numbers The 32 playable squares are numbered 1–32, starting at Black's lower-left corner. The move list uses them.
    • Men · เบี้ย move one square diagonally forward, and capture forward only.
    • Capture · กิน is compulsory. Finish every jump in a chain; pick any chain you like.
    • Promotion A man reaching the far row becomes a king (ฮอส · Hos); the move ends there.
    • King · ฮอส flies any distance diagonally, forward or back.
    • King capture from any distance, but it must land on the square directly behind the captured piece.
    • Win Capture all enemy pieces, or leave them without a legal move.
    • Draw By agreement; when the same position comes up three times; after 25 moves each without a capture or a man move; or when each side has only one king left and nobody can force a win.

    How to play

    1. Move Click a piece, or drag it. Dots show where it can go. Men move one square diagonally forward.
    2. You must capture Jump over an enemy piece whenever you can — it is compulsory. Keep jumping with the same piece while it can. Men only capture forward.
    3. Become a king Reach the far row and your man is crowned ฮอส. A king flies any distance along a diagonal, forward or back.
    4. King captures A king may jump from far away, but it must land on the square right behind the captured piece.
    5. Square numbers The 32 playable squares are numbered 1–32, starting at Black's lower-left corner. Moves are written with them, like 10-14 or 22×15.
    6. Play online Press Online in the top bar (on a phone: New, then Online) to make a room. Send the link or the code to a friend, and they can join from their own phone or computer.
    7. Win or draw Capture every enemy piece, or leave them with no legal move, to win. A draw is agreed, or happens when the same position comes up three times, after 25 moves each without a capture or a man move, or when each side has only one king left.
